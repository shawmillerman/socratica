Case Study: What's Driving Revenue?
Overview:
This project introduces you to a light business case study using fictional sales data from a made-up office supply company recently “donated” to PSU. In this fictional scenario, you and your partner will help the Dean of the School of Business make sense of the data and explore sales performance across product categories and regions.

You’ll use Excel to explore the data, identify patterns, and make a short recommendation based on your findings.

This assignment can be completed as a group assignment with no more than two people per group.
