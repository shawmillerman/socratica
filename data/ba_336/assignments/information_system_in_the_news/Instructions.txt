Overview
Information Systems don’t exist in a vacuum. They constantly evolve. And they are integral to modern business, technology, and society. In this assignment, you’ll explore a current news article related to information systems, analyze how the article relates to concepts we've covered in class, and hopefully fostering a deeper understanding of the subject.

Your Task
Article Selection: Find a recent article (published within the last 3 months) directly related to Information Systems. Relevant topics include cybersecurity, AI, digital platforms, SaaS, data privacy, blockchain, or other related areas.
Summary: Write a concise summary of the article (100–150 words) that captures its main points and relevance to Information Systems. 
In-Class Discussion: Bring your article and summary to class, where we'll discuss key takeaways from your articles, connect them to course ideas, and pose open-ended question for class discussion.

The full project guide, including detailed instructions for your individual summary expectations, and the grading rubric, can be found in the Information Systems in the News Assignment Guide
