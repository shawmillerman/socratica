Excel Project 2: Apartment Furnishing Budget

Overview:
Create a workbook to plan and track the cost of furnishing a new apartment. You’ll build individual room budgets, summarize total expenses, calculate a loan repayment plan, and visualize your spending, all while staying within a total budget of $5,000. This project applies concepts from Chapters 1–6, with a focus on formulas, formatting, and financial functions.

Group Assignment Modification (Optional)
This week's assignment might be completed as an in-class collaborative project divided into two specific phases.

Phase 1: Team Build (40 Minutes) You will work in assigned groups using a Driver/Navigator dynamic. One student will manage the Excel worksheet building (the Driver), while the remaining team member will guide the logic and verify compliance with the project rubric (the Navigators).

Phase 2: Peer Audit & Feedback (20 Minutes) After finishing the worksheet (or after 40 minutes), you will email your file to another team. You will spend this phase evaluating another group’s workbook. You are responsible for reviewing their formula logic, ensuring there are no hardcoded values where formulas belong, and checking the professional layout.

Submission Deliverables: Both students should have a copy of the Excel file. Include both student names in the naming of the file and submit the same Excel workbook.
