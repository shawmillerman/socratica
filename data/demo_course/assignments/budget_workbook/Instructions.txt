Budget Workbook

Overview: Build a one-month personal budget workbook in Excel or Google Sheets.

Requirements
- A sheet named Income listing at least two income sources with amounts.
- A sheet named Expenses listing at least eight expense lines grouped by category.
- A Summary sheet that uses formulas (SUM, and one IF or lookup) to show total income, total expenses and the remaining balance.
- A one-sentence note on the Summary sheet explaining what the balance means.

Submission: upload the workbook before the Week 2 deadline.
