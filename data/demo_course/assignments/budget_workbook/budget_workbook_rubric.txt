RUBRIC: Budget Workbook (50 points)

1. Structure (10 pts): Income, Expenses and Summary sheets exist and are clearly named.
2. Data (10 pts): At least two income sources and eight expense lines, grouped by category.
3. Formulas (20 pts): Totals use formulas, not typed numbers. At least one IF or lookup formula is used correctly.
4. Explanation (10 pts): The Summary note explains the balance in plain language.
