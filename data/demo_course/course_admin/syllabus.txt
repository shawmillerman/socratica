DEMO 101: Introduction to Data Literacy (sample course)

This is a small, original sample course that ships with Socratica so the app runs without any real course
materials. Replace it with your own course folder under data/.

Instructor: Sample Instructor
Format: 4 weeks, one short reading and one activity per week.

Learning goals
- Read a simple table of data and describe what it shows.
- Build a small budget workbook with formulas.
- Write a one-page memo that states a finding and supports it with a number.

Grading
- Budget workbook: 50 points
- Short memo: 50 points
- Late work loses 10% per day. Ask the instructor before the due date if you need an extension.

Academic integrity
Use Socratica to ask questions and get feedback on your approach. Submit only work you wrote yourself.
